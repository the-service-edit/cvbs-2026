CVBS QUOTE GENERATOR

Open CVBS-Quote-Generator.html in your browser. Works offline.

1. Tap your name under "Who is sending this?" (Karen, AJ, Chantelle, Rychelle).
   Your name, title, phone and email fill in, and the generator remembers you next time.
   Tip: bookmark the file with ?from=karen (or aj, chantelle, rychelle) on the end.
2. Choose the type of document:
   - One venue, costed: a full line-by-line estimate for ONE venue. Name the venue,
     then add or remove line items. Qty x unit and GST calculate live.
     Quoting three venues in full? Make one of these per venue.
   - Compare venues: one page putting up to three venues side by side with a
     headline "from" rate each. The first venue is marked as our pick.
3. Fill in the client and event details.
4. Click "Print / Save as PDF" and choose "Save as PDF". Each document fits one A4 page.

To add or change a team member's details, edit the TEAM list near the top of the script.
