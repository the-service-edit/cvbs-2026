## Instagram and Facebook (7 slides, 2160x2700)

**Caption**

We went to Crown Towers Sydney.

Three bookable event spaces in the whole building. The Pearl Ballroom holds 390
theatre and 340 for a banquet, and the other two hold forty each. Above them,
349 rooms, suites and villas, which means a conference and its room block sit in
one lift core.

It is a beautiful room for a senior audience. It is not a room for 500 people,
and we would rather tell you that now than at the shortlist.

Full record, with what Crown publishes and what it does not:
conferencevenues.com

## Karen's LinkedIn (7 slides, 2400x1256)

Draft for Karen to approve. First person, her judgement, no CTA.

> I spent a morning at Crown Towers Sydney.
>
> Here is the thing most people miss about that building. It is one of the most
> impressive rooms in the country, and it has exactly three bookable event
> spaces. The Pearl Ballroom takes 390 theatre. The other two hold forty people
> each.
>
> That is not a criticism. It is a brief. If you are running a board program, an
> incentive, an awards night for 300, the room does work you cannot buy any other
> way. If you are running a conference with four concurrent streams, no amount of
> marble fixes the arithmetic and I would send you to ICC.
>
> The other thing I would not have known from a website: Crown's two official
> sites publish different capacities for the same ballroom. Forty seats apart. If
> you are building a budget off the wrong page, you find out late.
>
> Thirty seven years of doing this and the answer is still the same. Go and
> stand in the room, then ring the venue and check the number.
