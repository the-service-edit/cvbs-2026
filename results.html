Case snapshots

A few briefs that came our way.

Indicative examples, with the client details left out. The outcomes are real.

Case snapshot

National conference, 600 delegates

The brief: A professional body needed a CBD venue with a plenary room, eight breakouts and 400 delegate rooms, in one of the busiest months of the year.

What happened: Found and secured inside a week, at a day delegate rate 14 per cent below the venue's published price, with the accommodation packaged into the same negotiation.

Case snapshot

Leadership offsite, regional

The brief: An executive team wanted somewhere to think properly, well away from the office, and they wanted it soon.

What happened: An exclusive-use property in wine country, confirmed within 48 hours with audio visual and private dining included, and still under the original budget.

Case snapshot

Product launch, 250 guests

The brief: A brand needed a space that looked the part for a flagship launch, with a difficult load-in and a demanding audio visual set-up.

What happened: Matched to a design-led venue, with the styling and audio visual partners coordinated around it as one managed booking.
