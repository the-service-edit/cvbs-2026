# Destination research register, 6 September 2026

The gaps behind the sixteen destination pages. Everything here was actively
looked for and could not be confirmed from the venue's own published material
or an official convention bureau, so it is **not on the site**. This file
exists so nobody researches the same dead end twice, and so Karen and AJ can
close the ones that only firsthand knowledge can close.

Source priority used throughout: the venue's own site and capacity chart, then
official brand documentation, then the official convention bureau. Directories
were used to discover venue names and never as the source of a figure.

---

## 1. WHERE AUTHORITATIVE DATA WAS INSUFFICIENT

**Ceiling heights are missing across most of the market.** 116 of the 190
venues in the dataset publish none. BCEC publishes no ceiling height for any
space at all, including the Great Hall. Every Hobart hotel except Crowne Plaza
publishes none. Wrest Point prints a ceiling column with no units and
implausible values (Tasman Room "41"), so it is nulled rather than guessed.

**Setup breakdowns are missing at most regional, winery and resort venues,**
which publish a single "up to N guests" figure without naming the layout.
Recorded against the layout the venue names, or not at all:

- Elements of Byron: capacity and square metres only, no theatre or banquet.
- Mindil Beach Casino Resort: floor areas only, no per setup capacities.
- Balgownie Estate, Meletos, Spicers Vineyards Estate, Byron Bay Surf Club:
  no capacities published anywhere.
- Peppers Moonah Links: capacity compendium blocked by robots.txt.
- Howard Smith Wharves: seated and standing only, for all seventeen spaces.
- Royal Exhibition Building: floor areas only, no setup capacities.
- Rochford Wines, Stones of the Yarra Valley, Healesville Sanctuary,
  d'Arenberg, Hunter Valley Gardens, Roche Estate, Bimbadgen, Elfin Hill.

**Conflicts inside a venue's own sources, resolved by taking the capacity
chart over the marketing page:**

- Sunshine Coast Convention Centre: the resort page says Minyama seats 1,500
  theatre; the centre's own chart says 1,000 theatre and 1,500 cocktail. We
  publish 1,000. **Worth one call to the venue.**
- Federation Ballroom, Hobart: Business Events Tasmania lists 1,700 theatre
  and 1,500 sqm; the venue publishes 1,200 and 1,225 sqm. We publish 1,200.
- Princes Wharf 1: the bureau lists 4,000 theatre; the venue says 60 to 1,200.
  We publish the banquet figure only.
- Hunter Valley Conference and Events Centre: the regional tourism authority
  says "up to 1,600 delegates"; the venue's chart says 1,160 theatre. We
  publish 1,160.
- The Star Brisbane Event Centre: the operator says 1,800 cocktail and
  theatre; the bureau says 2,000 theatre. We publish 1,800.
- QT Canberra Grand Ballroom classroom: kit says 336, web page says 400.
- Sofitel Melbourne on Collins: two Accor channels disagree on banquet,
  classroom, cocktail and cabaret, and one gives an impossible ballroom area.
  Only theatre, floor area and ceiling survive.
- Crystalbrook Riley: the collection page says 330 guests, Riley's own page
  says 420 for the combined zones.

**Venues left out entirely for lack of publishable data:** Melbourne Cricket
Ground, InterContinental Brisbane (mid two year transformation), Stamford
Plaza Adelaide, Emporium Hotel South Bank, RACV Noosa Resort, Ballina RSL,
Peninsula Hot Springs, Red Hill Estate, Port Phillip Estate, Charles Darwin
University, Darwin Entertainment Centre, MyState Bank Arena, Hobart City Hall.

**Whole-destination figures deliberately not published:** total hotel room
supply for any city. The available numbers are undated, differ in scope
between the bureau and the venue, and mix hotel rooms with serviced
apartments. Where room supply matters we name individual properties instead.

**Drive times.** Only figures published by a venue or an official authority
are on the site. Nobody publishes town to town drive times in the Hunter
Valley, the Blue Mountains, the Yarra Valley or the Sunshine Coast, so those
pages describe transfers as coached movements without inventing minutes.

**Seasonality we could not source and therefore did not publish:** Sunshine
Coast peak windows and recurring events, Yarra Valley vintage and bushfire
timing, Perth peak and shoulder conference seasons, Hunter Valley harvest
dates, Blue Mountains Yulefest, Byron Shire parking and event permits (council
returned 403 on every attempt).

## 2. REQUIRES KAREN, AJ OR CVBS FIRSTHAND KNOWLEDGE

None of the following can be published as firsthand without them, and none of
it is on the site now.

1. **The `seen` field is None on all 190 venues.** Not one venue on the site
   carries a firsthand inspection claim it has not earned. The five venues
   under `/venue-visits/` are the only firsthand content, and each one carries
   the date it was walked.
2. **The `worked` badge is set on twelve venues only**, all Sydney, all
   inherited from the pre-existing "venues we know inside out" list. **Nothing
   in the 132 new venues is marked as worked with.** If CVBS has booked any of
   them, Karen has to say so before the badge goes on.
3. **Any claim of a relationship, a negotiated outcome, a client experience or
   a venue drawback discovered on site.** The trade-offs on every page are
   destination level and sourced. Venue level judgement of that kind is CVBS's
   to add.
4. **Which of the fifteen new destinations CVBS actually books regularly.**
   The pages currently say "we source across the whole market", which is true
   and safe. If some destinations carry real depth, saying so would be the
   single strongest addition to this section.
5. **Whether Crowne Plaza Hunter Valley is still trading.** IHG's own
   reservation index returns no Hunter Valley property and the property's
   domain is a single thin page. Left off the site until someone confirms.
6. **Chateau Elan, Oaks Cypress Lakes and Balgownie total room counts.** None
   publishes one, and room count is the binding constraint in all three
   regions. One phone call each would close the biggest remaining gap on the
   Hunter Valley and Yarra Valley pages.

## 4. CURRENCY CORRECTIONS CARRIED INTO THE COPY

- 233 Victoria Square is **Amora Hotel Adelaide**, not Hilton Adelaide.
- The Byron at Byron is **Crystalbrook Byron**.
- Sofitel Noosa Pacific Resort is **Elysium Noosa**.
- Hilton Brisbane became **InterContinental Brisbane** in July 2025.
- **Victoria Park closed as a Brisbane event venue on 31 May 2026.**
- **Harvest Newrybar closed in December 2024.**
- **Wolgan Valley is absent from One&Only's current destination list.**
- Bluesfest and Splendour in the Grass are not running, so the old Byron Bay
  Easter and July blackout advice is gone.
- Hilton Darwin was the Crowne Plaza, not a DoubleTree. The DoubleTree at 122
  The Esplanade is now **Hilton Garden Inn Darwin**, and the separate
  **DoubleTree by Hilton Esplanade Darwin** still trades under that brand.
- Grand Hyatt Melbourne's event floor is listed by the hotel as closed for
  upgrade works with no reopening date.
- The Perth Convention and Exhibition Centre redevelopment was **discontinued
  by the WA Government on 6 November 2025**.
- Destination Gold Coast has rebranded to **Experience Gold Coast**.
