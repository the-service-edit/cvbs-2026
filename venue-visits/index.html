How a record is made

Nothing here is written by anyone who was not there.

The capacities are the venue's own

Read off each venue's published capacity chart, fact sheet or technical specification, never a directory and never estimated.

The photographs are ours

Taken by whoever was in the building. Not supplied by the venue and not stock, which is the whole reason a record is worth reading.

We check the published numbers

Venues routinely publish figures that contradict each other across their own websites. Every record says which source we used and where the numbers disagree.

Fit, not fault

We will tell you where a room stops being comfortable and who we would send you to instead. What we will not do is score a venue out of ten.
