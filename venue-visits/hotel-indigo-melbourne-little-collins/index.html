Hotel Indigo Melbourne Little Collins

Hotel288 Little Collins Street, Melbourne VIC 3000

  • 12Boardroom
  • 6Private dining
  • 179Rooms
  • 10Floors

Inspected by CVBS

A 179 room lifestyle hotel over ten floors on Little Collins Street, opened in 2025 and sharing its building with a Holiday Inn. One boardroom for twelve, and no conference space. Rooms run 24 to 40 square metres.

What it suits

Good for

  • Delegate room blocks in the middle of the Melbourne CBD
  • Board meetings and executive briefings to 12
  • Private dinners for 4 to 6 in the winter garden
  • The accommodation half of a program with its plenary elsewhere
  • Individual corporate travellers on a rate program

Look elsewhere for

  • Any conference, plenary or breakout program
  • Anything above 12 people in one room
  • Gala dinners and awards nights
  • Exhibitions or anything needing floor load

Accommodation

179 rooms over ten floors. Standard rooms are 24 to 34 square metres, premium rooms 32 to 40, and one bedroom suites run 38 to 86. Above those sit the Bijou and Solitaire suites, the latter with a private balcony and a circular bath.

Where these figures come from, and why a dash is not a zero

A dash means the hotel publishes no figure for that setup. It does not mean the room cannot be set that way, only that we will not print a number the venue has not published.

Recent changes

Opened 2025. IHG announced it on 15 September 2025 as its first dual branded hotel in Australia, opening alongside Holiday Inn Melbourne Bourke Street Mall in the same complex.

It shares an address with a second hotel. Holiday Inn Melbourne Bourke Street Mall occupies the same complex and has 273 rooms and a 220 sqm terrace event space. If a proposal quotes event space at this address, it is worth confirming which of the two hotels it belongs to.

Questions organisers ask

What size event can it hold?

Twelve. The hotel publishes one meeting space, a boardroom on level two given as 12 guests, and a private dining conservatory for four to six. There is no conference space.

How many rooms does it have?

179 rooms over ten floors. Standard rooms are 24 to 34 square metres, premium rooms 32 to 40, and one bedroom suites 38 to 86.

Can it hold a conference?

No. For a plenary in Melbourne, the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre’s Plenary seats 5,540, Crown Melbourne’s Palladium 1,500 theatre and Sofitel Melbourne On Collins’ Grand Ballroom 1,150.

What is it actually good for?

A delegate room block in the middle of the Melbourne CBD, a board meeting for twelve, or the accommodation half of a program whose plenary runs somewhere else.

When did it open?

In 2025. IHG announced it on 15 September 2025 as its first dual branded hotel in Australia, opening alongside Holiday Inn Melbourne Bourke Street Mall in the same complex.
