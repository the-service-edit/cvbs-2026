Crown Towers Sydney

Hotel390 theatre340 banquet390 cocktail349 rooms

1 Barangaroo Avenue, Barangaroo NSW 2000

Inspected by CVBS

Event floor 3 photographsAccommodation 4 photographsRest of the building 6

A 349 room hotel at the northern tip of Barangaroo, and the shortest way to understand it for an event is this: Crown publishes three bookable spaces, and two of them hold forty people. Everything above a private dinner happens in one room, the Pearl Ballroom, which seats 390 theatre style and 340 for a banquet.

It is not a conference venue and it does not pretend to be one. What it is, is the best room in Sydney to put a senior audience in when the building itself is doing part of the persuading. Board programs, incentive and reward, awards nights, client entertaining, a 300 person conference that wants plenary and beds in the same lift core. Above 390 in one room, or with more than two concurrent streams, this is the wrong building and we would put ICC Sydney or Shangri-La in front of you instead.

Where these figures come from, and why a dash is not a zero

A dash means Crown publishes no figure for that setup. It does not mean the room cannot be set that way, only that we will not print a number the venue has not published.

Every figure is read from Crown's own Pearl Ballroom capacity panel, checked 5 September 2026. Never estimated, never taken from a directory. Crown publishes no floor area for any of its Sydney spaces, which is why the area column is empty throughout. For the Opal Suite it publishes “meetings 20” without naming a setup, and for both small rooms it publishes “seated events”, which we have read as banquet.

The two Crown websites disagree. crownhotels.com.au carries the full table above under a 2026 copyright. crownsydney.com.au still says the ballroom holds 350, under a 2019 to 2022 copyright. We use 390.

The event floor

The Pearl Ballroom is the only room in the building that takes more than forty people, so every conference, gala and awards night here happens in it. An expansive Pre-Function Foyer runs alongside it, and the ballroom divides into three for smaller events.

Accommodation

Group accommodation is half of what we do at a building like this, and it is the half a floor plan tells you nothing about. Crown Towers Sydney has 349 rooms, suites and villas above the event floor, which means a conference and its room block can sit in one lift core.

What we ask before holding a block here

  • Room block release terms and the attrition point on a 100 room hold
  • How many rooms sit on each floor, and how many lift cars serve them at 7am
  • Breakfast seating and turn time, which decides whether 250 delegates make a 9am start
  • Which room types can be held as a single block rather than split across categories
  • Whether event delegates and leisure guests share the same check in desk on a Friday
  • Accessible room count and their location relative to the event floor

If you need the beds without the ballroom, that is a normal brief and we run it on its own. See group accommodation for how a room block is held, and corporate accommodation for an ongoing rate program.

What Crown does not publish

These are the questions we put to the venue, and the answers go on this page once we have them. On a building of this profile the list is unusually long.

  • Pearl Ballroom floor area in square metres, and its length by width
  • Clear ceiling height in the ballroom, under any beam or rig
  • Whether the ballroom is pillarless, and where any columns fall
  • The names and individual capacities of the three Pearl divisions
  • Pre-Function Foyer capacity, which Crown publishes for no setup at all
  • U-shape, boardroom and hollow square figures, which are published nowhere
  • Rigging points, floor load and the loading dock run to the ballroom
  • A real floor plan. The asset labelled "Floorplan of Pearl Ballroom" on Crown’s own venue page is a map of Barangaroo, not a plan of the room.

What it suits

Good for

  • Board and executive programs where the room is part of the argument
  • Incentive and reward programs, 20 to 300
  • Awards nights and gala dinners to 340
  • Conferences to 300 that want plenary and beds in one building
  • Client entertaining in the Opal Suite or poolside at The Pavilion
  • Residential programs built around Sydney Harbour as the backdrop

Look elsewhere for

  • Plenaries above 390 in a single room
  • More than two concurrent breakout streams. Crown publishes three spaces and two of them hold 40
  • Exhibitions, or anything needing floor load and rigging signed off
  • Programs that need floor areas and ceiling heights confirmed early
  • Budget sensitive programs. This is the top of the Sydney market

Recent changes

February 2026. Oncore by Clare Smyth, the restaurant on level 26 that opened with the building and carried much of its profile, served its last dinner on 28 February 2026. A replacement had not been named at the time of the announcement.

Now trading. Golden Century has reopened inside Crown Sydney under the Wong family, and Crown has announced Én by Björn Frantzén for 2027. Any dinner or private dining proposal quoted off an older Crown Sydney page should be checked against the current restaurant list before it goes to a client.

The two Crown websites disagree on the ballroom. crownsydney.com.au still says Pearl Ballroom holds 350 guests. crownhotels.com.au publishes the full capacity table and says 390. A proposal built off the older page is 40 seats short before anyone has walked the room.

Questions organisers ask

What size event can it hold?

Pearl Ballroom seats 390 theatre, 340 for a banquet, 320 for a banquet with a stage, 280 cabaret, 125 classroom and 390 for a cocktail reception. It is the only large room in the building. The Opal Suite and The Pavilion each hold 40 for a cocktail reception.

How many event spaces are there?

Three bookable spaces, plus the Pre-Function Foyer beside the ballroom. The ballroom divides into three, but Crown publishes no capacity for the divisions, so we cannot tell you what a third of the room holds without asking.

Is there accommodation on site?

Yes, 349 rooms, suites and villas in the same building as the event floor.

What is the ballroom’s floor area?

Crown does not publish it, and it does not publish a ceiling height or the room’s dimensions either. The file labelled “Floorplan of Pearl Ballroom” on Crown’s own venue page is a map of Barangaroo, not a plan of the room. If your production needs a plan, we get it from the venue directly.

Is it big enough for a large conference?

Not above 390 in one room. ICC Sydney’s Grand Ballroom takes 2,000 for a banquet and 3,500 for a cocktail reception, its Parkside Ballroom 1,400 theatre, its Gallery 1,080 banquet, Shangri-La Sydney’s Grand Ballroom 700 theatre and Sofitel Sydney Darling Harbour’s Magnifique Ballroom 450 theatre.
