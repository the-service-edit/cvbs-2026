Fairmont Singapore

Hotel80 Bras Basah Road, Singapore 189560

  • 2,257Ballroom sqm
  • 1,800Ballroom banquet
  • 34Meeting rooms
  • 778Rooms

Inspected by CVBS

A 778 room Fairmont in Raffles City, in the middle of Singapore’s Civic District. Its largest room is the Fairmont Ballroom, 2,257 square metres with a 6 metre ceiling, for 3,000 theatre or 1,800 at banquet. Two more ballrooms and 34 meeting rooms in all make up Raffles City Convention Centre, the event floor it shares with the Swissôtel in the same complex.

It reaches shortlists for scale. It can hold a plenary in the thousands, feed the same group at dinner and sleep most of them upstairs, with the MRT underneath.

What it suits

Good for

  • Conferences of up to 3,000 theatre style with the delegates staying upstairs
  • Gala dinners and awards nights of up to 1,800 seated
  • Two or three events at once across three ballrooms
  • Programs with many breakouts, with meeting rooms from 44 to 183 square metres
  • Delegates arriving by train, with City Hall MRT under the complex

Look elsewhere for

  • A dinner above 1,800, which needs a convention centre ballroom
  • A small board or incentive group that wants a boutique hotel feel
  • A program that wants a resort setting rather than the middle of the city

Accommodation

778 rooms and suites, each with a private balcony, and a club lounge on the upper floors.

Questions organisers ask

What size conference can it hold?

Up to 3,000 theatre style or 1,800 at banquet in the Fairmont Ballroom, a 2,257 square metre room with a 6 metre ceiling, by the hotel’s capacity chart. It divides into three rooms, Padang, Canning and Collyer, of 754 square metres and 900 theatre each.

What else is there besides the main ballroom?

Two more ballrooms. The Stamford Ballroom is 810 square metres and takes 850 theatre or 480 banquet, and divides into two rooms of 325 theatre. The Atrium Ballroom takes 400 theatre or 320 banquet. Below those sit meeting rooms from 44 to 183 square metres, 34 rooms in all by the hotel’s count.

How many guest rooms does it have?

778 rooms and suites, each with a private balcony, by the hotel’s count. That houses a large conference under one roof, with a club lounge for delegates on the upper floors.

Where is it?

At 80 Bras Basah Road in Raffles City, in the Civic District between Marina Bay and the colonial district. The event floor is Raffles City Convention Centre, which the hotel shares with the Swissôtel in the same complex.

Who does it suit?

A large association or corporate conference that wants the plenary, the breakouts, the gala dinner and the delegates in one building, in the middle of the city. It also suits running two or three events at once across its three ballrooms.
