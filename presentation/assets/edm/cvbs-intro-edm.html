I wanted to introduce myself properly. I am Karen, and along with Anthony, Chantelle and Rychelle I run CVBS. We find venues for conferences, meetings, incentives and corporate events, and we have been doing it since 1989.

If you have ever had to source a venue yourself, you will know how the week goes. Twenty websites, a dozen enquiry forms, quotes that arrive in different formats, and a rate you have no way of knowing is a good one. Send us the brief instead and we will do that part. You get a shortlist of options that actually suit the event, in one proposal, usually within 48 hours.
