Why we would send you here anyway

The Westin sits on levels 32 to 46 of Asia Square, which means every session runs with the South China Sea, Marina Bay and the city skyline behind it. There are 305 rooms across sixteen levels and the standard deluxe is 48 square metres, which is generous for Singapore. The event spaces share those floors with the restaurants, the bars and the poolside terrace, so a day can move from a session to a drink without anyone leaving the building. Delegates who have been flying since morning will be glad of the Heavenly beds.
