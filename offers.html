The Westin Singapore

Marina Bay, Singapore

Levels 32 to 46 of Asia Square, in the heart of Marina Bay, with 305 rooms and event spaces looking out over the bay and the city.

The offer
5% off your master bill plus double Bonvoy points

On conferences, meetings and events booked by 30 December 2026 with a spend above USD 15,000. Room rates start at SGD 330, Marriott Bonvoy points double for the organiser, and every 30 rooms you book earns a night free.

Enquire about this offer

There are not many conference hotels where delegates come back from the break early just to stand at the window. The Westin Singapore runs from level 32 to level 46 of Asia Square in Marina Bay, so the South China Sea, the bay and the city skyline are with your group all day. There are 305 rooms, a deluxe room that starts at 48 square metres, and restaurants, bars and a poolside terrace for the part of the day that isn't the agenda.

What the offer includes

On group and catering bookings, or group only, above USD 15,000.

  • Room rates from SGD 330 a night
  • 5% off your master bill
  • Double Marriott Bonvoy points
  • One complimentary room night for every 30 rooms booked

What your delegates get

  • A deluxe room from 48 square metres, with a view over the bay or the city
  • The Heavenly Bed, which is the reason people ask to stay here again
  • Wellness and fitness facilities, and a poolside terrace to unwind on
  • Restaurants and bars in the building, so an evening does not need a taxi

Terms and conditions apply. Valid on bookings made by 30 December 2026 with a spend above USD 15,000. Blackout dates 8 to 11 October 2026 and 31 December 2026. Hotel rooms, room rates and conference spaces are subject to availability and to restrictive blackout dates.

voco Darwin Suites

Darwin, NT

Darwin's newest premium stay, in the middle of the CBD, with apartment-style suites and event spaces built around natural light.

The offer
Pick your perk on every part of the booking

Choose a free inclusion on your full day delegate package, another on a plated dinner, and take special group rates with a free room for every 15 you book. New bookings booked and held before 28 February 2027.

Enquire about this offer

voco Darwin Suites has just opened in the middle of the CBD, and the thing your delegates will notice is space. Every suite has a separate living area and kitchen facilities, so three nights at a conference stops feeling like three nights in a hotel room. Mitchell Street's dining, the Darwin Waterfront and the shopping are all an easy walk, and the event spaces have natural light, current audiovisual and a team who will run the day alongside you. It works as well for a board strategy session as it does for a long-table dinner.
