Hyatt Place Melbourne, Caribbean Park

Melbourne, VIC

171 rooms and suites set on 275 acres of parkland, 27 km from the Melbourne CBD, with naturally lit event spaces.

The offer
5,000 Hyatt points on every event

Every event earns 5,000 World of Hyatt points, plus one free extra chosen from the list below. Group room bookings earn separately. New bookings held 23 June to 31 August 2026.

Enquire about this offer

Hyatt Place Melbourne, Caribbean Park sits on 275 acres of parkland 27 km from the city, with 171 rooms and suites and naturally lit event spaces. Rewards apply to the event and to your delegates’ rooms, so a residential conference earns on both sides.

Booking an event

Every event earns 5,000 World of Hyatt points, plus one free extra.

Spend $5,000 or more, choose one

  • Fresh juice shots on arrival
  • Care station
  • Self-serve lolly bar

Spend $10,000 or more, choose one

  • 30-minute cocktail event
  • Snack station
  • A post-conference drink per guest

Booking group rooms

On 20 or more room nights.

  • Up to 30,000 World of Hyatt points
  • A free room for every 20 booked
  • VIP amenities for your guests
  • A room upgrade for your organiser

T&Cs apply. New bookings held 23 June to 31 August 2026. World of Hyatt points by tier: 10,000 for 20 to 29 rooms, 20,000 for 30 to 39, 30,000 for 40 or more. One free room per 20 booked and materialised. VIP amenities capped at 10. Upgrades subject to availability.
