When your people travel often, or a project puts a team in one city for months, accommodation becomes a recurring cost and a recurring hassle. We set up negotiated corporate rates at properties that suit the way your people travel, then manage the bookings as one relationship, so it stays predictable and on budget.

What we handle

  • Negotiated corporate and long-stay rates
  • Consistent, vetted properties for repeat travel
  • Project-team and relocation accommodation at scale
  • Centralised booking, billing and reporting
