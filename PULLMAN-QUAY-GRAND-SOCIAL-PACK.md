# Pullman Quay Grand: the post

Drafted 27 Aug 2026 from Mel's 31 October 2025 photographs. Facts checked against
`PULLMAN-QUAY-GRAND-DRAFT-ENTRY.md`.

## The call

This is not a site visit carousel. Six of the ten photographs are inside a bar that
closed four weeks after they were taken, none of them shows a meeting room, and the
visit is ten months old. Posted as "we went and looked" it is stock photography
with a stale date on it.

Posted the other way round it is the best argument for the index we have had all
year. The venue changed. Our record carries the date someone was there. That is
the FIRSTHAND position proved with a receipt rather than asserted.

So the post is about what changed, not about the lunch.

Nothing here criticises the hotel. Accor refit its harbourfront bar and built a new
restaurant, which is what a good hotel does. The point is the shelf life of a
capacity chart, not the venue.

## The one fact the whole post turns on

Mel photographed Hacienda on **31 October 2025**. Hacienda closed. **Acapulco El
Vista opened in the same room on 28 November 2025**, four weeks later. **Flaminia**
opened a floor below on **5 December 2025**.

Four weeks. That number is the post.
