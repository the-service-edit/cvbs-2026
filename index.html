The process

How your event comes together.

01

You tell us about your event

Dates, delegate numbers, where you would like to be and roughly what you have to spend. Use the form or call and talk it through, whichever is easier for you.

02

We go looking

We check what is really available across the venues that suit your brief, including rooms that never show as free online, then negotiate rates and inclusions on your behalf.

03

You compare real options

Within 48 hours you have a shortlist you can compare. Rates, inclusions, capacities and the trade-offs between them, set out side by side.

04

We confirm and stay with it

We lock in the rate, handle the contract detail and coordinate accommodation and suppliers through to the day. The decisions stay yours throughout.

Why organisers use us

We would rather send you a handful of venues we can vouch for than a list you have to work through.

Doing it yourself means chasing availability, paying the published rate and reconciling quotes across your inbox. With us, your brief goes to someone who knows the General Managers by name, has a good sense of which rooms work for what, and can negotiate rates and inclusions that never appear online. You get a shortlist within 48 hours, and a person to talk it through with.
