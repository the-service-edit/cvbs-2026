What a day delegate package normally covers

  • The plenary room for the day, set the way your program needs it
  • Arrival coffee, morning and afternoon tea, and lunch
  • A screen, a projector and a basic microphone
  • Notepads, pens, water and the usual room stationery

Breakout rooms, exhibition space, dinners and anything beyond the house audio visual sit outside the package and are quoted separately.

What moves the number

  • Your delegate numbers, which is the single biggest lever
  • The month and the day of the week you are asking for
  • Whether accommodation and a dinner come with it
  • How much of the venue you are taking, and for how many days

A conference with rooms attached is a much stronger piece of business for a venue than a room hire on its own, and the rate usually reflects that.

Published rates are a starting point, not the price. Tell us your dates and numbers and we will come back with what those venues will actually do for an event like yours.

Direct or through us

Why not just ring the venues yourself?

You certainly can, and for a familiar room on flexible dates that is a perfectly sensible thing to do. Here is what changes on a conference once the dates, the delegate numbers and the accommodation start pulling against each other.

Going direct

  • You work from published rates and the venues that came up in a search
  • Every enquiry, chase and revised quote is yours to make
  • Quotes arrive in different formats and are hard to compare properly
  • You find out about the difficult load-in or the low ceiling on the day
  • Room hire, catering, audio visual and accommodation negotiated separately

Through CVBS

  • We check what is genuinely available, including rooms that never show as free
  • One brief from you, and we do the ringing around
  • One shortlist, costed the same way, so you can compare like for like
  • You hear the drawbacks at shortlist, not once the contract is signed
  • The whole package negotiated together, which is where the rate moves

FAQ

Conference venue finding, common questions

The questions organisers actually ask us before they brief.

Does it cost more to use CVBS for a conference venue?
No. Our service is free to you, and the rate we negotiate often comes in under what you would be quoted on your own. If you would like the detail, it is set out on how we’re paid.
What is a fair day delegate rate?
It depends on the city, the standard of the venue and what your program actually needs, so the honest answer is a range rather than a number. The bands we see across the market are set out further up this page, and the fuller version is in our conference cost guide.
How large a conference can you source for?
From small board meetings to large multi-day conferences, including programs with concurrent breakouts, exhibition space and group accommodation. If a venue cannot hold the whole program in one place, we will tell you that before you inspect it.
How quickly will I get options?
A shortlist within 48 hours of your brief, with rates and inclusions ready to compare. If your dates are tight or the market is busy we will say so when you brief us rather than at the 48 hour mark.
How far ahead should we be booking?
For a large conference in a capital city, twelve to eighteen months is comfortable and the good rooms in peak season go earlier than that. Shorter lead times are workable more often than people expect, particularly if you have any flexibility on the week. Tell us the dates you want and we will tell you honestly what is still open.
Do you only put forward venues that suit you?
No. We source right across the market rather than for any one hotel group, and the venues that reach your shortlist are the ones that fit the brief. You will also hear what is wrong with each of them, because that is usually the part that decides it.
Who handles the contract and the deposits?
The contract is between you and the venue. We negotiate the terms that sit inside it, including the inclusions, the deposit schedule and the release dates, and we stay across it through to the final invoice.
What happens if we have to move or cancel the dates?
Tell us as early as you can and we will go back to the venue. Because we place a lot of business with the same properties, a date move is very often something that can be worked out rather than a cancellation, and that conversation is easier coming from us than from a one-off booker.
